# AI-security research writing

Apply only to AI-security research and its deliverables.

- Distinguish documented facts, observed behavior and our assessment. State uncertainty
  where evidence is missing; do not turn a vendor claim into an established capability.
- Check material claims against primary sources. Scope conclusions to the product,
  deployment and evidence actually examined; keep version details with those findings.
- Use plain, specific language. Explain the mechanism and consequence instead of relying
  on impressive labels, filler intensifiers or sweeping claims.
- Avoid repeated slogan-like contrasts, epigram closers and formulaic section summaries.
  Prefer direct statements; do not enforce arbitrary bans on ordinary useful words.
- Use "we" for our work, "my assessment" for owned judgment, and attributed third person
  for external claims. Avoid an anonymous institutional voice.
- Keep detail that supports a decision or makes a claim checkable. Remove repetition,
  generic background and recommendations that do not follow from the evidence.

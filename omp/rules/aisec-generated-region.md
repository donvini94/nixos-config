STOP. You are editing a GENERATED region. Your change will be destroyed by the next
`node reports/ai_security_matrix/gen.mjs`, and the data store will still be wrong.

Scores, counts, holes, plane and area projections, the Acts split and the vendor census
are all generated from `reports/ai_security_matrix/data/`. Edit the store, then run:

    node reports/ai_security_matrix/gen.mjs
    node reports/ai_security_matrix/check.mjs

Only prose OUTSIDE the `#+BEGIN:`/`#+END:` and `<<gen:>>` markers is hand-written.
